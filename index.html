Understanding the Existing System

Pathao is a multi-service digital platform supporting services such as Ride, Food, Parcel, and Courier. This academic study analyzes the system from a System Analysis and Design perspective and identifies areas where reliability, usability, transparency, and operational efficiency can be improved.

5 Core Problems Identified

01

Inconsistent Application Performance

Slow loading, delayed responses, temporary lag, and inconsistent responsiveness can negatively affect real-time service usage.

Impact
  • Reduced user confidence
  • Slower transactions
  • More customer complaints
02

Location Tracking & Map Accuracy

Incorrect pickup locations, GPS inaccuracies, and delayed map updates can create coordination problems between customers and providers.

Impact
  • Longer waiting times
  • Wrong pickup points
  • Reduced operational efficiency
03

Booking Reliability & Cancellation

Delayed provider acceptance, cancellations after confirmation, and prolonged waiting can make booking unreliable.

Impact
  • Schedule disruption
  • Customer frustration
  • Reduced trust
04

Fare Transparency & Pricing Confusion

Users need clearer information regarding estimated fares, additional charges, and differences between estimated and final prices.

Impact
  • Pricing confusion
  • Customer disputes
  • Reduced confidence
05

Customer Support & Complaint Resolution

Slow complaint handling and limited case visibility can reduce customer satisfaction.

Impact
  • Unresolved cases
  • Higher support pressure
  • Reduced customer loyalty

Is the Improvement Practical?

Technical Feasibility

HIGH

Most proposed improvements can be implemented through software optimization, GPS/map integration, improved algorithms, monitoring, and database enhancements.

Operational Feasibility

HIGH

The existing user workflow remains largely familiar, reducing user adaptation and operational disruption.

Economic Feasibility

FEASIBLE

Incremental enhancement requires investment but avoids the much larger cost and risk associated with completely replacing the existing platform.

Implementation Roadmap

Fare Transparency

Customer Support

Continuous Monitoring

Evaluate performance metrics, complaint patterns, cancellation rates, location issues, and feedback.
